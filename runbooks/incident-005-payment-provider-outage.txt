Title: Payment failures due to third-party processor outage
Date: 2025-10-05
Severity: Critical

Summary:
Payment success rate dropped from 98% to 40% within minutes. No deploys
had happened in the previous 24 hours, which initially ruled out a
code-related cause.

Root cause:
Our external payment processor was having a partial outage on their end.
Our payment service had no circuit breaker, so it kept retrying failed
calls to the degraded provider, which also slowed down unrelated requests
due to thread pool exhaustion.

Fix:
Added a circuit breaker that stops calling the payment provider after a
threshold of failures, and shows users a "try again shortly" message
instead of hanging. Set up a status page subscription for the provider
to get outage alerts directly.

How to recognize this pattern:
Sudden failure rate increase with zero recent deploys usually points
outward, not inward - check third-party/vendor status pages before
digging through your own code or logs.
